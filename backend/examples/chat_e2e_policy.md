# Coastal Heat Resilience Grant 2027

## Purpose

The Coastal Heat Resilience Grant helps Australian local councils reduce heat exposure in public
spaces. Projects must provide measurable community benefit and prioritise neighbourhoods with high
heat vulnerability, limited tree canopy, and large populations of older residents or young children.

## Funding and eligibility

The program has a total budget of AUD 48 million. An eligible local council may request between
AUD 250,000 and AUD 2 million. Joint applications by two or more councils may request up to
AUD 3.5 million. Universities and private companies may participate as project partners, but only an
Australian local council may serve as the lead applicant and receive grant payments.

## Application timetable

Applications open on 1 December 2026 and close at 5:00 pm Australian Eastern Daylight Time on
15 March 2027. Late applications will not be accepted. The department expects to notify successful
applicants by 30 June 2027, and funded projects must begin before 1 October 2027.

## Eligible activities

Funding may support shade structures, drinking-water stations, cool pavements, urban tree canopy,
heat-safe playground upgrades, community heat refuges, and public heat-warning systems. Ordinary
road maintenance, retrospective costs, political advertising, and projects located outside Australia
are not eligible.

## Reporting

Recipients must submit progress reports every six months and a final outcomes report within 90 days
after project completion. Reports must include expenditure, delivery milestones, canopy or shade
coverage where relevant, and evidence of changes in community heat exposure.
