# OECD.AI Demo Questions / OECD.AI 演示问题

## A. Single-document retrieval / 单文档事实检索

### 1. Australia: policy structure / 澳大利亚：政策结构

中文：澳大利亚 AI 行动计划的四个重点领域是什么？请分别概括每个领域对应的政策目标，并引用原文页码。

English: What are the four focus areas of Australia's AI Action Plan? Summarise the policy objective of each area and cite the relevant pages.

### 2. Australia: SME support / 澳大利亚：中小企业支持

中文：这份计划如何帮助中小企业采用 AI？请列出 National AI Centre、AI and Digital Capability Centres 以及区域项目的具体作用和相关预算。

English: How does the plan help small and medium-sized enterprises adopt AI? Describe the roles of the National AI Centre, the AI and Digital Capability Centres, and the regional programme, including their relevant budgets.

### 3. Australia: talent / 澳大利亚：人才

中文：澳大利亚为缓解 AI 人才短缺提出了哪些措施？请区分本土教育培训、产业合作和全球人才吸引措施。

English: What measures does Australia propose to address AI talent shortages? Distinguish between domestic education and training, industry collaboration, and global talent attraction measures.

### 4. Singapore: strategy structure / 新加坡：战略结构

中文：新加坡 NAIS 2.0 的三个系统、十个赋能要素和十五项行动之间是什么关系？请用层次清晰的方式说明。

English: How are the three systems, ten enablers, and fifteen actions in Singapore's NAIS 2.0 related? Explain their hierarchy clearly.

### 5. Singapore: public-sector adoption / 新加坡：公共部门应用

中文：新加坡计划怎样扩大公共部门的 AI 应用？请概括应用场景、跨部门资源支持和公务员 AI 能力建设。

English: How does Singapore plan to expand AI adoption in the public sector? Summarise the proposed use cases, cross-government resource support, and AI capability building for public officers.

### 6. Singapore: enabling infrastructure / 新加坡：基础设施

中文：新加坡如何把算力、数据和可信环境作为 AI 战略基础？请分别说明政策工具及其预期作用。

English: How does Singapore use compute, data, and a trusted environment as foundations of its AI strategy? Explain the policy instruments and intended role of each.

## B. Cross-document comparison / 跨文档比较

### 7. Business adoption / 企业采用

中文：比较澳大利亚和新加坡促进企业采用 AI 的政策路径。两国分别使用了哪些机构、资金、能力中心或场景驱动机制？

English: Compare Australia and Singapore's policy approaches to promoting business adoption of AI. What institutions, funding, capability centres, or use-case-driven mechanisms does each country employ?

### 8. Talent strategies / 人才战略

中文：比较两国的 AI 人才战略：它们如何培养本地人才、吸引国际人才，并把人才与产业或研究需求连接起来？

English: Compare the two countries' AI talent strategies. How do they develop local talent, attract international talent, and connect talent with industry or research needs?

### 9. Innovation and trustworthy AI / 创新与可信 AI

中文：两份战略如何处理“创新”与“可信、负责任 AI”之间的关系？请只依据文档，区分明确行动、原则性承诺和仍未说明的实施细节。

English: How do the two strategies balance innovation with trustworthy and responsible AI? Use only the selected documents and distinguish between concrete actions, principle-level commitments, and implementation details that remain unspecified.

### 10. Implementation readiness / 实施可操作性

中文：从实施可操作性角度比较两份战略。哪些措施给出了明确预算、执行机构、时间范围或可量化目标，哪些仍主要停留在战略愿景层面？

English: Compare the two strategies in terms of implementation readiness. Which measures specify budgets, responsible bodies, timeframes, or measurable targets, and which remain mainly at the level of strategic vision?

## C. Response modes / 回答模式

### 11. Researcher mode / Researcher 模式

中文：请比较两国推动公共部门和私营部门采用 AI 的政策工具，并按 Relevant Cases、Key Lessons、Risks、Implementation Considerations 和 Practical Recommendations 组织答案。建议只能复述并注明来自文档的建议。

English: Compare the policy instruments used by the two countries to promote AI adoption in the public and private sectors. Structure the answer under Relevant Cases, Key Lessons, Risks, Implementation Considerations, and Practical Recommendations. Only restate recommendations that appear in the documents and attribute them explicitly.

### 12. Policymaker mode / Policymaker 模式

中文：请为政策讨论准备一份简短对照：两国在中小企业支持、人才、算力、数据和治理方面分别采取了什么做法？不要替我选择政策方案，并明确标出证据缺口。

English: Prepare a concise comparison for a policy discussion: what approaches does each country take to SME support, talent, compute, data, and governance? Do not choose a policy option for me, and clearly identify evidence gaps.

### 13. Student mode / Student 模式

中文：假设我是第一次学习 AI 政策，请用简单语言解释澳大利亚和新加坡为什么都要投资 AI 人才和基础设施，以及它们的方法有什么不同。

English: Assume I am learning about AI policy for the first time. In simple language, explain why Australia and Singapore both invest in AI talent and infrastructure, and how their approaches differ.

## D. Evidence-boundary test / 证据边界测试

### 14. Unsupported outcome comparison / 缺乏证据的成效比较

中文：根据这两份文件，哪一个国家的 AI 政策已经在 2025 年带来了更高的全国劳动生产率？请给出具体提升百分比。

English: Based on these two documents, which country's AI policy had produced a larger increase in national labour productivity by 2025? Provide the exact percentage increase.

预期表现：系统应明确说明两份文件没有提供足够的 2025 年事后评估数据，而不是自行推断或编造百分比。

Expected behaviour: The system should state that the two documents do not provide sufficient ex-post evaluation data for 2025, rather than inferring or inventing a percentage.

## Best three questions for a short demo / 短演示首选三问

优先使用第 2、9、14 问，分别展示精确事实与数字检索、跨文档综合比较，以及证据不足时的安全拒答。

Use Questions 2, 9, and 14 to demonstrate precise retrieval of facts and figures, cross-document synthesis, and safe handling of insufficient evidence.
