# OECD.AI Demo Reference Answers / OECD.AI 演示参考答案

本答案集仅依据以下两份本地政策文件编写，不代表对政策实际成效的独立评估：

- [Australia's AI Action Plan (2021)](pdf/australia-ai-action-plan-2021.pdf)（简称 **AUS**）
- [Singapore National AI Strategy 2.0 (2023)](pdf/singapore-national-ai-strategy-2.0-2023.pdf)（简称 **SGP**）

This answer key is based only on the two local policy documents above. It is not an independent evaluation of policy outcomes.

**页码说明 / Page convention:** `PDF p.` 指文件中的物理页序；`printed p.` 指页面上印刷的页码。引用优先给出 PDF 页序，便于系统检索结果复核。澳大利亚文件中的 `$` 按原文件表述；新加坡文件明确出现的金额使用 `S$`。不同币种及不同年份的金额不应直接比较。

## 1. Australia: policy structure / 澳大利亚：政策结构

### 中文参考答案

计划包含四个重点领域：

1. **推动企业开发和采用 AI**：帮助企业利用 AI 创造就业、提高生产率和竞争力。
2. **培养并吸引世界级 AI 人才**：确保企业能够获得高水平人才和专业知识。
3. **用前沿 AI 解决国家级挑战**：把研究能力用于经济、健康、社会、基础设施和环境问题，并让更广泛的人群受益。
4. **建设负责任且包容的 AI 环境**：增强公众信任，使 AI 反映澳大利亚价值观并扩大包容性参与。

计划把直接 AI 措施、数字技能项目和基础政策设置放在这四个领域下组织；总览页进一步列出了各领域对应的项目。[AUS, PDF pp.7-8; printed pp.5-6]

### English reference answer

The plan has four focus areas: helping businesses develop and adopt AI; growing and attracting world-class AI talent; applying advanced AI to national challenges; and making Australia a leader in responsible and inclusive AI. Their objectives are respectively to improve business productivity and competitiveness, give firms access to expertise, translate research into solutions with broad public benefit, and build trustworthy AI consistent with Australian values. [AUS, PDF pp.7-8; printed pp.5-6]

## 2. Australia: SME support / 澳大利亚：中小企业支持

### 中文参考答案

- 政府为 **National AI Centre 与 4 个 AI and Digital Capability Centres 合计投入 5,380 万**，文件没有把这笔金额进一步拆分到各中心。
- National AI Centre 设在 CSIRO Data61，负责协调全国 AI 专长、统筹各能力中心、促进产业与研究合作，并处理中小企业采用和开发 AI 时遇到的障碍。
- 4 个 Capability Centres 面向具体 AI 应用，为中小企业提供进入 AI 生态的“前门”，包括专家、工具、设施、咨询、培训以及与研究人员的连接。
- **Catalysing the AI Opportunity in our Regions** 获得 **1,200 万**，通过区域企业、研究机构和技术公司联合申请的竞争性配套资助，把 AI 用于区域问题。项目设计为 3 轮，每轮最多 12 项，每项资助 25 万至 100 万。
- 相关但范围更广的数字化支持还包括投入 1,270 万、面向 17,000 家小企业的 Digital Solutions 咨询服务；它不是前述 5,380 万中心预算的一部分。

[AUS, PDF p.14, printed p.12; PDF pp.25-26, printed pp.23-24]

### English reference answer

The government allocates **$53.8 million in total** to the National AI Centre and four AI and Digital Capability Centres; the document does not split that amount between them. The National AI Centre, located within CSIRO's Data61, coordinates expertise, aligns the Capability Centres, connects research and industry, and addresses SME adoption barriers. The four Capability Centres give SMEs access to specialists, tools, facilities, advice and training. A separate **$12 million** regional programme co-funds three grant rounds, with up to 12 grants per round worth **$250,000-$1 million** each. A broader Digital Solutions service also allocates $12.7 million for advice to 17,000 small businesses. [AUS, PDF p.14, printed p.12; PDF pp.25-26, printed pp.23-24]

## 3. Australia: talent / 澳大利亚：人才

### 中文参考答案

**本土教育与培训：**Next Generation AI Graduates 投入 2,470 万，通过高校和产业共同资助的奖学金培养至少 234 名本土、可直接就业的 AI 专才；学生参加产业课题、实习和职业训练。另有 2,260 万的 Emerging Technology Graduates、1,070 万的 Digital Skills Cadetship，以及 Job-Ready Graduates、JobTrainer 和学徒补贴等更广泛的技能渠道。

**产业与研究合作：**AI 研究生项目把研究题目与产业需求连接；ARC Linkage 等项目促进高校、企业和公共研究机构合作。文件称自 2018 年起 ARC 已向涉及 AI 的项目、合作和研究职位投入超过 2 亿；University of Adelaide 的 Centre for Augmented Reasoning 获 2,000 万，并通过博士奖学金支持人才与研究。

**全球人才吸引：**Global Business and Talent Attraction Taskforce 主动吸引顶尖学者、研究人员和专家，以填补关键技能缺口，并把技术、研发、知识产权和资本带到澳大利亚。所引段落没有给出该 Taskforce 的独立 AI 预算。

[AUS, PDF pp.16-18, printed pp.14-16; PDF p.27, printed p.25]

### English reference answer

Domestically, the $24.7 million Next Generation AI Graduates programme uses university- and industry-co-funded scholarships, industry projects and placements to train at least 234 job-ready AI specialists. Broader pathways include emerging-technology scholarships, digital cadetships, Job-Ready Graduates, JobTrainer and apprenticeships. Industry links are reinforced through ARC Linkage projects and the Centre for Augmented Reasoning. Internationally, the Global Business and Talent Attraction Taskforce targets leading academics, researchers and experts to fill skills gaps and bring technology, R&D, IP and capital to Australia; the cited passage gives no separate AI budget for that taskforce. [AUS, PDF pp.16-18, printed pp.14-16; PDF p.27, printed p.25]

## 4. Singapore: strategy structure / 新加坡：战略结构

### 中文参考答案

NAIS 2.0 是三层关系：**系统**定义国家 AI 生态的三大组成部分，**赋能要素**说明每个系统靠什么运转，**行动**则把这些要素转化为未来 3-5 年的具体政策任务。

| 系统 | 赋能要素 | 对应行动 |
|---|---|---|
| System 1: Activity Drivers | Industry、Government、Research | 1-2：企业 CoE 与初创生态；3：公共部门采用；4：国家 AI 研发计划 |
| System 2: People & Communities | Talent、Capabilities、Placemaking | 5-6：顶尖人才与 15,000 名实践者；7-8：企业采用与劳动力培训；9：AI 社群场所 |
| System 3: Infrastructure & Environment | Compute、Data、Trusted Environment、Leader in Thought and Action | 10：算力；11-12：数据能力与公共数据；13-14：治理、安全与韧性；15：国际合作与规则塑造 |

因此，15 项行动不是与 10 个赋能要素并列的另一套分类，而是分布在这些赋能要素下的执行组合；战略还说明会随着 AI 的快速变化持续复核行动。[SGP, PDF p.16, printed p.14; action detail at PDF pp.23-63]

### English reference answer

The three systems are the top-level architecture; the ten enablers describe what makes each system work; and the fifteen actions operationalise those enablers over the next three to five years. System 1 contains Industry, Government and Research and Actions 1-4. System 2 contains Talent, Capabilities and Placemaking and Actions 5-9. System 3 contains Compute, Data, Trusted Environment, and Leader in Thought and Action and Actions 10-15. The actions are therefore nested under the enablers, not a separate parallel taxonomy, and the strategy says they will be reviewed as AI develops. [SGP, PDF p.16, printed p.14; action detail at PDF pp.23-63]

## 5. Singapore: public-sector adoption / 新加坡：公共部门应用

### 中文参考答案

新加坡从三个层面扩大公共部门 AI 应用：

- **应用场景：**通用场景包括面向市民服务的智能转录、写作、数据分析等；专业场景包括金融报表和交易异常检测、医疗、教育等。OneService chatbot 用 AI 分类市政投诉并分派机构；Pair 为公务员提供在政府 IT 环境内安全使用大模型的工具。
- **组织与资源：**Smart Nation 重点领域由具备专业知识、技术和监管工具的机构制定部门 AI 战略；财政、人力资源和服务交付等跨政府职能则由牵头机构识别流程。中央层面提供资金、跨机构数据共享、高性能算力与工程能力，并发布便利实施的政策指南。
- **公务员能力：**政府计划提高所有公务员的基础 AI 素养，并按高级管理、政策和业务岗位推出有针对性的课程。

[SGP, PDF pp.26-28, printed pp.24-26]

### English reference answer

Singapore combines concrete use cases, whole-of-government support and workforce training. General uses include transcription, drafting and data analysis, while specialised uses include anomaly detection for anti-money-laundering work and sector applications in healthcare and education. OneService classifies and routes municipal reports, and Pair lets officers use LLM capabilities inside government systems. Central support includes funding, cross-agency data sharing, high-performance compute, engineering support and enabling guidelines. Targeted courses are planned for senior management, policy and operational roles. [SGP, PDF pp.26-28, printed pp.24-26]

## 6. Singapore: enabling infrastructure / 新加坡：基础设施

### 中文参考答案

**算力：**战略把可靠、本地化的高性能算力视为产业、科研和政府创新的必要条件。政策工具包括与芯片企业和云服务商深化合作、为数据中心安排电力和碳资源、规划绿色数据中心，以及由政府管理少量 GPU，用于能力建设、创新和公共利益项目。

**数据：**重点从单纯“获得更多数据”转向数据质量、情境适配和可信共享。工具包括发展隐私增强技术（PETs）、研发和监管沙箱、数据治理能力，以及研究设立政府“data concierge”，帮助发现和协调公共数据；对高价值私营数据场景，政府可作为可信中介。

**可信环境：**政府提出适应技术变化、支持创新但有护栏的治理方式。具体工具包括更新 Model AI Governance Framework 和 AI Verify、开展标准化测试、监管沙箱、水印和模型卡试点、分层且基于风险的行业规则，以及企业安全指南、技术标准和联合红队测试。

[SGP, PDF pp.49-60, printed pp.47-58]

### English reference answer

For compute, Singapore seeks reliable local high-performance capacity through partnerships with chipmakers and cloud providers, resource planning for data centres, a longer-term green data-centre roadmap, and a small managed pool for capability-building and public-good uses. For data, it prioritises quality and trusted, context-appropriate sharing through PET research, sandboxes, guidance and a possible government data concierge. For trust, it combines updated governance frameworks and AI Verify with testing, sandboxes, risk-tiered rules, security guidance, standards and red-teaming. [SGP, PDF pp.49-60, printed pp.47-58]

## 7. Business adoption / 企业采用

### 中文参考答案

澳大利亚采用较明确的**机构和资助驱动**路径：National AI Centre 负责全国协调，4 个 Capability Centres 为中小企业提供专家、工具和培训；区域项目用竞争性配套资助推动地方场景；CRC-P 促进企业与研究机构合作。关键金额包括中心体系 5,380 万、区域计划 1,200 万，以及自 2018 年起面向 21 个产业主导 AI 项目的 4,370 万。[AUS, PDF p.14, printed p.12; PDF pp.25-26, printed pp.23-24]

新加坡更强调**行业场景和企业成熟度分层**：在企业和行业层面建设 AI Centres of Excellence，由 Industry Champions 形成需求；通过加速器和 AI Trailblazers 推动快速试验；企业可使用 AIRI 评估准备度，基础阶段使用 SMEs Go Digital、CTO-as-a-Service 和 Industry Digital Plans，成熟企业则通过 Digital Leaders Programme 获得定制支持。AISG 的 100 Experiments 还能为企业问题匹配公共研究人员，每项目共同资助最高 S$330,000。[SGP, PDF pp.19, 22-25, 31, 40-42; printed pp.17, 20-23, 29, 38-40]

两者共同点是连接企业、研究和政府资源；主要差异是澳大利亚文件给出更多集中预算与区域资助细节，而新加坡文件更突出行业 CoE、场景需求、企业成熟度和生态编排。由于币种、年份和项目口径不同，文件不能支持直接比较投入规模。

### English reference answer

Australia follows a comparatively explicit institution-and-funding model: a National AI Centre coordinates the ecosystem, four Capability Centres give SMEs access to expertise and facilities, regional grants fund local use cases, and CRC-P links industry with researchers. Singapore uses a more sector- and maturity-based model: corporate and sectoral CoEs, Industry Champions, accelerators, AI Trailblazers, AIRI readiness assessment, SMEs Go Digital, CTO-as-a-Service, Industry Digital Plans and tailored Digital Leaders support. AISG's 100 Experiments also co-funds industry problem-solving with public researchers up to S$330,000 per project. The documents do not support a direct spending comparison because currencies, years and programme scopes differ. [AUS, PDF p.14 and pp.25-26; SGP, PDF pp.19, 22-25, 31, 40-42]

## 8. Talent strategies / 人才战略

### 中文参考答案

澳大利亚以**奖学金、产业项目和研究合作**培养本土人才：2,470 万的 AI Graduates 项目计划培养至少 234 名 AI 专才，并通过产业课题和实习连接就业需求；ARC Linkage、Centre for Augmented Reasoning 和更广泛的数字技能项目补充研究与培训。全球人才方面，Global Business and Talent Attraction Taskforce 定向吸引顶尖学者、研究人员和专家。[AUS, PDF pp.17-18 and 27; printed pp.15-16 and 25]

新加坡把人才分为 **Creators、Practitioners 和 Users**。它计划设专门团队吸引顶尖创造者，探索跨国混合工作、产业与学术兼职和国际机构合作；把 AI Practitioners 扩大到 **15,000 人**，扩展 AI Apprenticeship Programme、职前教育、继续教育和企业实习，同时继续接纳全球技术人才。企业采用项目、行业培训、AI CoE、研究合作及实体 AI 社群场所负责把人才与产业、研究和创业需求连接起来。[SGP, PDF pp.35-45; printed pp.33-43]

差异在于：澳大利亚文件对若干人才项目给出较具体的预算、人数和实施批次；新加坡给出更系统的人才分层和 15,000 人目标，但多数行动未列独立预算。

### English reference answer

Australia develops local talent through funded scholarships, industry projects, placements and research-industry links; its AI Graduates programme allocates $24.7 million and targets at least 234 specialists. Its global channel is the Global Business and Talent Attraction Taskforce. Singapore distinguishes Creators, Practitioners and Users: it proposes a dedicated team and flexible arrangements to attract leading Creators, aims to raise the Practitioner pool to 15,000, scales apprenticeships and education pipelines, and remains open to global talent. CoEs, sector training, R&D collaboration and an AI community site connect people to industry and research. Australia supplies more programme-level budget and cohort detail; Singapore supplies a broader workforce architecture and a headline Practitioner target. [AUS, PDF pp.17-18 and 27; SGP, PDF pp.35-45]

## 9. Innovation and trustworthy AI / 创新与可信 AI

### 中文参考答案

**澳大利亚的明确行动：**落实包含 8 项原则的 AI Ethics Framework；以 OECD AI Principles 为国际承诺基础；审查 Privacy Act；制定 Australian Data Strategy；建立公共部门数据安全、透明共享的设置；通过多元人才措施提升包容性。[AUS, PDF pp.21-23; printed pp.19-21]

**新加坡的明确行动：**把 Responsible AI 作为基础研究重点；持续更新 Model AI Governance Framework 和 AI Verify；以测试、监管沙箱、水印、模型卡和标准把原则转化为工具；按风险和应用领域分层治理；更新网络安全工具包并推进安全标准和红队测试。[SGP, PDF p.20 and pp.56-60; printed p.18 and pp.54-58]

**原则性承诺：**澳大利亚强调创新、可信、人权、民主价值和包容性；新加坡强调“支持试验与创新，同时确保负责任使用”，并保持监管敏捷性。

**仍未说明的实施细节：**澳大利亚文件未给出所有伦理原则的强制执行机制、统一量化指标或违规处置流程；新加坡文件也未为每类风险规定具体阈值、法律责任、全面预算或完成日期。因而可以说两份战略都试图把信任视为创新和采用的条件，但不能仅据这两份文件断言哪国治理更有效。

### English reference answer

Australia's concrete measures include implementing its eight-principle AI Ethics Framework, aligning with the OECD AI Principles, reviewing privacy law, developing a national data strategy and establishing safer public-sector data-sharing settings. Singapore goes further into operational tooling through the Model AI Governance Framework, AI Verify, testing, sandboxes, watermarking and model-card pilots, risk-tiered governance, security guidance, standards and red-teaming. At principle level, both treat trust as enabling adoption and innovation. However, the documents do not fully specify enforcement, risk thresholds, legal accountability, complete budgets or outcome metrics, so they cannot establish which approach is more effective. [AUS, PDF pp.21-23; SGP, PDF p.20 and pp.56-60]

## 10. Implementation readiness / 实施可操作性

### 中文参考答案

澳大利亚文件的实施细节更集中：直接 AI 措施总投资 **1.241 亿**，并列出 5,380 万中心体系、1,200 万区域项目、2,470 万 AI 研究生项目和 3,370 万国家挑战项目；中心和首轮项目计划在 2021-22 启动，区域资助分布至 2023-24，人才项目也列出批次；CSIRO/Data61、CSIRO、高校、产业伙伴等执行主体较明确。区域项目还给出轮次、项目数和单项资助范围。[AUS, PDF pp.14, 17, 20 and 24-27; printed pp.12, 15, 18 and 22-25]

新加坡提出 **未来 3-5 年 15 项行动**，可量化内容包括把 AI Practitioners 扩大至 **15,000 人**；部分既有案例也有量化信息，例如 AI Trailblazers 的 100 天 100 个生成式 AI 场景、100 Experiments 每项目最高 S$330,000。文件能识别政府、IMDA、AISG、行业负责人和各机构的角色，但多数新行动没有集中预算、年度里程碑或统一成效指标，一些还使用“探索”“评估可行性”等措辞。[SGP, PDF pp.16, 19, 23-32, 37-42 and 49-63]

所以，就这两份战略文本本身而言，澳大利亚在预算和时间安排上更“项目化”；新加坡在系统设计、责任方向和能力目标上较清晰，但相当一部分仍需后续实施文件。两份文件都主要是前瞻计划，不能用来证明实际效果已经实现。

### English reference answer

Australia is more programme-specific in this document: it identifies $124.1 million in direct AI measures, itemised programme budgets, delivery bodies, 2021-24 milestones, grant rounds and grant sizes. Singapore defines fifteen actions over three to five years and includes a target of 15,000 AI Practitioners, while some cited existing initiatives have numerical details. However, many new Singapore actions lack consolidated budgets, annual milestones or common outcome measures, and some remain exploratory. On textual readiness, Australia is more budget-and-timeline specific, while Singapore is clearer as a system architecture; neither document proves realised outcomes. [AUS, PDF pp.14, 17, 20 and 24-27; SGP, PDF pp.16, 19, 23-32, 37-42 and 49-63]

## 11. Researcher mode / Researcher 模式

### 中文参考答案

**Relevant Cases：**澳大利亚以 National AI Centre、4 个 Capability Centres 和区域配套资助推动私营部门采用，并用政府挑战项目形成应用需求。新加坡以企业和行业 CoE、AI Trailblazers、100 Experiments、SMEs Go Digital 推动企业采用，以 OneService、Pair 和跨政府职能改造推动公共部门采用。[AUS, PDF pp.14, 20 and 25-26; SGP, PDF pp.19, 23-31 and 40-42]

**Key Lessons：**从两份文件的设计可以归纳出，采用政策不是单一补贴：还需要可获得的专家、数据、算力、培训、真实问题和研究合作。澳大利亚通过全国协调中心和区域资助组织这些要素；新加坡则通过行业场景、企业成熟度和公共部门示范形成需求。这是对文件设计的归纳，不是经过成效评估验证的因果结论。

**Risks：**澳大利亚文件指出技能短缺、SME 采用障碍、信任和隐私问题；新加坡文件还强调算力稀缺、资源消耗、数据安全、偏差与错误输出、恶意使用、网络攻击及就业扰动。[AUS, PDF pp.14, 16 and 21-23; SGP, PDF pp.40 and 49-60]

**Implementation Considerations：**需要明确协调机构和实施主体，为试验提供资金、数据、算力和工程能力；按岗位和行业培训人员；同时建立隐私、治理、测试和安全机制。文件还显示，区域和中小企业可及性以及公共部门跨机构协调是实施中需要专门处理的问题。

### English reference answer

**Relevant Cases:** Australia uses a National AI Centre, four Capability Centres, regional co-funded grants and government challenge programmes. Singapore uses corporate and sectoral CoEs, AI Trailblazers, 100 Experiments and tiered enterprise programmes, while OneService, Pair and whole-of-government transformation support public adoption.

**Implementation Considerations:** The documents point to clear coordinating bodies, resources for experimentation, role- and sector-specific training, cross-agency coordination, and privacy, testing, governance and security controls.

**Practical Recommendations:** No independent recommendation is added here. Australia proposes a package of coordinating and capability centres, grants, research-industry collaboration and an ethics framework. Singapore proposes CoEs, use-case experimentation, maturity-tiered enterprise support, central public-sector resources, PETs, AI Verify and risk-based governance. ## 13. Student mode / Student 模式

### 中文参考答案

AI 不会只靠软件自己产生价值。企业和政府需要会设计、部署和监督 AI 的人，也需要算力、数据和安全规则。如果缺少人才，工具不会被正确使用；如果缺少基础设施，再好的人才也难以训练和运行系统；如果公众不信任系统，采用也会受阻。

澳大利亚的方法比较像建设一组明确的项目：给奖学金和研究合作资金，建立全国中心和能力中心，并为区域企业提供资助。新加坡的方法更像搭建一套完整生态：把人分为创造者、实践者和使用者，把产业、政府和研究组织起来，同时专门规划算力、数据和可信环境。两国都连接人才与实际需求，但澳大利亚文件更常给出项目预算和批次，新加坡文件更突出系统架构、行业场景和规模目标。[AUS, PDF pp.7-8, 14 and 16-18; SGP, PDF pp.16, 35-42 and 49-60]

### English reference answer

AI needs people who can build, use and oversee it, plus compute, data and safety rules. Without skills, organisations cannot use the tools well; without infrastructure, even skilled teams cannot develop and run systems; without trust, people and businesses may resist adoption. Australia presents a set of funded programmes, scholarships, centres and regional grants. Singapore presents a more explicit ecosystem linking Creators, Practitioners and Users with industry, government, research, compute, data and trust. Australia provides more programme budgets and rounds, while Singapore emphasises system design, sector use cases and scale targets. [AUS, PDF pp.7-8, 14 and 16-18; SGP, PDF pp.16, 35-42 and 49-60]

## 14. Unsupported outcome comparison / 缺乏证据的成效比较

### 中文参考答案

**无法根据这两份文件回答哪国政策在 2025 年带来了更高的全国劳动生产率，也无法给出具体提升百分比。**澳大利亚文件发布于 2021 年，新加坡文件发布于 2023 年；两者都是前瞻性战略，虽然把生产率提升列为目标或预期收益，但没有提供可归因于这些政策的 2025 年全国事后评估、反事实基线或可比较百分比。澳大利亚文件还表示将在可能情况下逐年衡量进展，这同样不是成效结果。[AUS, PDF pp.7 and 24, printed pp.5 and 22; SGP, PDF pp.20, 22, 27-28 and 35-42]

### English reference answer

**The two documents cannot establish which country achieved a larger national labour-productivity increase by 2025, and they provide no defensible percentage.** They are forward-looking strategies published in 2021 and 2023. Although both describe productivity as an objective or expected benefit, neither contains a comparable 2025 ex-post evaluation, counterfactual baseline or attributable national percentage. Such a claim would require 2025 official statistics, implementation data, comparable baselines and an evaluation design that separates AI-policy effects from other economic factors. [AUS, PDF pp.7 and 24, printed pp.5 and 22; SGP, PDF pp.20, 22, 27-28 and 35-42]
